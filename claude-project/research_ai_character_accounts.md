# Research 2: AI character accounts, what's out there and what works (17 Nov 2026)
Source artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/P1Cku8Gd3D1HhUwKxFVp8D. This is a survey of 34 AI influencer and AI character accounts on TikTok and Instagram, done to inform Halcyon Interns (Cyan and Mark). The data is a snapshot from 17 Nov 2026, pulled through Treg.

## Key findings
1. **One look, one bit.** The breakout characters are one recognisable person doing one repeatable thing. Jean Phil had no dialogue at all, only a handlebar moustache and shadowboxing. He got 1.5M then 5.6M views in his first week.
2. **Contrast is the hook.** A glam grandma talking money, or a scruffy old man in a hoodie leaving VIP venues with bodyguards. An overdressed intern in a three-piece suit has this built in. @mick.brennan72 got 14.8M views at 27K followers.
3. **It's a hits game.** Medians sit far below averages, so one video carries an account. Posting more buys more chances: Granny Spills posts about 2 to 3 times a day on TikTok (Instagram: 88.5K median, 4.68M average).
4. **Formats burn out, characters last.** Template trends die fast. A baby podcast account got a 12.2M hit and now gets about 700 views a post.
5. **Series bring people back.** Fruit Love Island runs 2 to 6 minute episodes with audience voting and still averages 468K. The original account got 3M followers in about 9 days.
6. **Viewers accept AI when it gives them something.** On characters that entertain or say something true, comments engage with the content. One example: "i get it's AI but she's speaking facts" (37K likes).
7. **The money is in what the account sells.** TikTok's Creator Rewards excludes fully AI content, and brand partnerships with AI accounts are down 30% vs 2024. A brand-owned account like Halcyon Interns already fits the model that works.

## The eight kinds of account
- **Personality-bit character:** one person, one repeatable bit (Granny Spills, Mick Brennan, Jean Phil). This is the closest model for Cyan.
- **Virtual lifestyle persona:** AI model posting fashion, fitness or daily life (Lil Miquela, Fit Aitana, Bia). Reach is steady but small.
- **Creature or animal vlogger:** yeti, monkey or street cat selfie vlogs (@highonyeti, @patican.ai, Babloo).
- **Animated cast or series:** recurring characters in episodes (Fruit Love Island, Glorb, Shelmi).
- **Fake-real formats:** street interviews, news anchors, caught-on-camera clips (@bingetelevision, @the.worldai).
- **Immersive history POV:** "you wake up as..." (@avbootcamp, @timeportals). These had the highest medians in the survey.
- **Workplace skits:** office humour that personifies software (@theaifilmmaker, @versojobs). These are close references.
- **Tool or course-led:** the character is the ad (Glorify, @mariaxelise).

## What performs
- A strong character archetype with a twist, so viewers get the joke in the first second.
- Short and punchy for personas (Granny Spills averages 12s, Baddie Betty 7 to 8s). Longer for stories (history POV 30s, Fruit Love Island 2 to 6 minutes).
- Relatable pain points, like workplace skits where the comments turn into a group therapy session.
- A recurring cast and numbered parts ("pt.4", "Episode 55", "Season 2").
- Riding a moment, for example Lil Miquela reacting to Stranger Things ending.

## What falls flat
- Pretty but empty personas (10K to 23K medians).
- Riding one template, which spikes and then collapses.
- Volume without a hook (cat melodrama at "Episode 55" with a 5.7K median).
- A big follower count with no fresh idea.
- Borrowed faces and IP (real celebrities, TV parodies, cartoon characters), which get reach but carry takedown risk.

## Risks and rules
- **Label realistic AI.** TikTok requires labels on realistic AI content, and since 21 July 2026 on realistic AI in ads. TikTok says the label doesn't affect distribution. Turn on the AI toggle and say "AI characters" in the bio.
- **Finance plus AI faces can read as a scam.** Never promise returns, never show made-up P&L as real, and tie every video back to the real firm.
- **No real people, no borrowed IP.** Cyan and Mark are original characters, which is an advantage worth protecting.
- **Don't count on Creator Rewards.** Fully AI content is excluded. The value for Halcyon is awareness and sign-ups.
- **Volume without originality gets downranked.** Original scripts and a consistent cast keep the account safe.
