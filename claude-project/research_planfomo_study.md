## What makes the format work
The text earns the views and the background only sets the mood. Every breakout gives the viewer something to keep. Saves of 4 to 10% of views push these posts far past the account's follower count.
- **Saveable payload.** Glossaries, rules, settings and ranked lists get saved and shared. Pure motivation lines get far fewer saves.
- **A twist or a reread.** "Now read it backwards" makes people watch twice, which is why a 7-second post reached 123.8k.
- **Short and loopable.** Most hits run 6 to 15 seconds, so the text gets read on the second loop. Longer text walls work when the first line is a strong claim.
- **Calm, dark b-roll.** A desk, charts, rain or a city at night. Nothing in the background competes with the words.
- **Plain white type.** Top half of the frame, soft shadow, no emojis on the video.
- **Tiny captions.** Two to six words plus one or two hashtags. The caption never repeats the on-screen text.
- **Sound.** Original or slowed ambient tracks, which keep the mood quiet.
- **Niche words in the title.** "Futures", "NQ", "order flow", "prop firm" and "PO3" show up in the biggest futures posts, so specific vocabulary pulls the right audience.
